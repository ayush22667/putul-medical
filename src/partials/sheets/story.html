Our Story

A shop of small bottles, and the weight they carried.

Long before it had a signboard worth photographing, this pharmacy was a single wooden counter in Binodpur, tended by a man who knew every family on the road by the ailments they came to him with — a fever here, a fading eyesight there, a child's first cough. He did not sell medicine so much as he kept a promise: that what he handed across that counter would be exactly what it claimed to be, in a town where that was never guaranteed.

That promise outlived him. It passed to his son, and then to the next, each generation adding what the last could not — better stock, fairer prices, a delivery boy on a bicycle who knew every lane in Katihar by heart. What never changed was the counter's quiet logic: a pharmacy is not measured by what it sells, but by whether the people who walk in trust that it will not let them down.

OUR VISION

We are not building a bigger shop. We are building the next counter — one that reaches every home in Katihar and Purnea, at any hour, without ever losing the face-to-face trust the first counter was built on.
